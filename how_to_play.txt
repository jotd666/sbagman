Main goal:

Get the bags of all 5 screens in the barrow before timer reaches 0. Then find the key and 
free your pal who's locked up at screen 2.

Hazards:

- The guards kill you on contact
- A fall from a height greater than your height kills you
- Elevators kill you if they fall onto you or move up when you're not completely inside it
- The mine carts will kill you unless you can grab handles. In that case, you can even drop down to the cart
- When you're in the cart, you are invincible, unless an elevator falls on you :)
- When you're carrying bags, you move slower. When carrying the blue bag, you move even slower
- Guards can shoot you as soon as you shot one guard, or from act 2
- Bombs can kill you if you're not far enough after having dropped them

Assets:

- use the mine carts to safely & quickly carry bags (specially the blue ones)
- elevators help you to cross screens with the barrow and to reach some otherwise unreachable areas
  They also are fast and helpful when carrying heavy bags.
- you need the pickaxe to destroy the wall that protects the blue bag,
  but you can also use a bomb.
- you can use the pickaxe to frighten/kill the guards (does not work when you're on a ladder)
- blocking a guard's path on the ladder when on top of it with the pick or the barrow causes the guard to fall down
- dropping a bag onto a guard in a ladder or a slope also knocks then down,
  but the drawback is that the bag must be carried up again.

General tactics:

- When you get the gun, you get six shots, and the guards get six shots each as well.
  If they're out of bullets, they can't shoot until you get another gun.
- At Act 1, avoid to shoot the guards unless absolutely necessary, as now guards shoot at
  you on sight (if they've still got bullets)
- When the guards are not in your screen, they are guided to your screen automatically.
- When the guards are not in your screen, they also never wait for elevators
  You can use that to divert them from elevators by switching screens when you're waiting for a free elevator
- Do not kill the guards by hitting them with the pickaxe: you'll lose it. Use it on top of ladders to make them
  fall, or to scare them into elevator shafts or holes.
- The higher score you get, the fastest the guards run. On the ladders, they always have the same speed.
- The elevators moves are now independent. That wasn't the case in Bagman, where you could control them by
  switching screens at the proper time. But it's not really a problem because
  of the new layout.  
- When carrying pickaxe for a long distance, drop it from time to time to avoid timeout
- When dropping the last bag (on screen 3) in the barrow, the barrow will remain there for the next level,
  which makes the start of the next level difficult. Getting the barrow on screen 2 or even 1
  when there's only one bag left can be a nice option (leave 1 or 2 bags high enough on screen 1/2, so you can
  quickly come back to screen one, pick & drop 2 last bags quickly, and when next level starts the barrow
  is next to you at screen one again! It's rather easy as the guards need time to get back at screen 1
  so you're alone)
- Once a score of 40000 is reached, the guards are as fast as the player, and faster when player carries
  a bag. This & trickier bag locations makes second level very difficult to complete. Can be done, though,
  but at that point the game wants you dead. Note that each completed level awards an extra life.
